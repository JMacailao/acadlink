Why AcadLink

Built for how Philippine schools actually run.

Most schools juggle a grading spreadsheet, an enrollment form, a cashier's book and a separate LMS. AcadLink puts them on one foundation: a student is enrolled once, assessed once, graded once, and every office reads the same record. Students follow it in the Student Portal, parents see their children's grades and balances, and the school keeps every record on its own server.

01

One account for everyone

Registrar, cashier, teachers, librarian, canteen staff, students and parents sign in once. Each person sees only the workspaces their role allows.

02

One record, every office

Admission becomes enrollment, enrollment becomes an assessment, payments post to accounting, and grades flow into the transcript, with no retyping.

03

Every action accounted for

An activity log, approvals for voids and refunds, roles and permissions, and backups power.admin can restore part by part.

Assessed once. Paid anywhere.

Learn in class and online

Classes, quizzes and attendance in one place.

Each class gets a stream, classwork, quizzes that check themselves, attendance and a gradebook. Students hand in files, text or links from any phone.

  • Auto-scored quizzes and exams
  • Private teacher–student messages

Step 04

Grade with confidence

From class work to the official grade.

Teachers send LMS averages to the Grading System as drafts, review them, and submit. The registrar tags terms complete, and grades appear in the Student Portal and the transcript.

  • School grade scale and transmutation
  • Grade changes with approval
  • Transcripts and certificates

Step 05

Canteen and library

Campus services on the same account.

The canteen sells by barcode or tap with shifts, cash counts and stock. The library checks books out by barcode, with holds, renewals, fines and stocktakes.

  • Canteen sales post to accounting
  • Library fines added to the student's balance
  • Due-date reminders by notification

Step 06

Parents stay informed

Read-only, and only their own children.

The registrar links parent accounts to students. Parents see released grades, class work, attendance, balances and receipts, and get notified when something changes.

  • One parent account for brothers and sisters
  • Same grade-release rules as the student
  • Notifications by bell and email

Records kept safe

Backed up, part by part.

  • Safety copy before every restore
  • Activity log of every change

Security & privacy

Your school's data, protected.

Security is part of every screen, not an add-on. Access follows roles, sensitive actions need approval, and every change is logged.

Roles & permissions

Groups for registrar, cashier, accounting, teachers, librarian, canteen and parents. View or edit, per module.

Activity log

Who did what and when: sign-ins, payments, grade changes, exports and restores.

Data Privacy Act tools

Privacy notice and consent at admission, "Download my data" for students, and retention reviews.

Backup & restore

Full or partial backups by power.admin, with a safety copy before every restore.
